### Corn_(maize)___Cercospora_leaf_spot_Gray_leaf_spot
classifier_label: Corn_(maize)___Cercospora_leaf_spot_Gray_leaf_spot

**What it looks like:** small, rectangular, tan-to-gray lesions running
parallel to the leaf veins, usually starting on lower leaves and moving
upward as the season progresses. Favors warm, humid weather and fields
with heavy crop residue on the surface.

**General management:**
- Rotate out of maize for at least one season where severe.
- Tillage/residue management to reduce leftover infected debris on the
  soil surface (the fungus survives on old maize residue).
- Resistant hybrids exist — ask your KVK which hybrids are recommended
  locally for this disease.
- Fungicide timing and product should come from local advisory if disease
  pressure is high during grain fill.

### Corn_(maize)___Common_rust_
classifier_label: Corn_(maize)___Common_rust_

**What it looks like:** small, oval, reddish-brown raised pustules
scattered on both leaf surfaces, which can turn blackish as the plant
matures. Usually most visible from mid-season onward.

**General management:**
- Most modern maize hybrids have at least partial resistance — resistant
  hybrid choice is the primary long-term control.
- Rust generally does not require fungicide treatment in resistant hybrids
  unless pressure is unusually severe; confirm with your agriculture
  officer before treating.

### Corn_(maize)___Northern_Leaf_Blight
classifier_label: Corn_(maize)___Northern_Leaf_Blight

**What it looks like:** long, cigar-shaped, grayish-green to tan lesions
(several cm long) running parallel to the leaf, usually starting on lower
leaves. Can cause significant yield loss if it reaches the upper leaves
before grain fill finishes.

**General management:**
- Resistant hybrids are the most effective control — ask your KVK which
  hybrids resist this disease locally.
- Crop rotation and residue management reduce carryover between seasons.
- Fungicide application timing (if needed) should be decided locally based
  on disease pressure and growth stage.

### Corn_(maize)___healthy
classifier_label: Corn_(maize)___healthy

**What it looks like:** uniform green leaves, no lesions or pustules,
normal growth for the crop stage.

**General management:** No action needed. Continue normal monitoring,
especially after humid weather, since several maize leaf diseases start on
lower leaves and are easy to miss early.
